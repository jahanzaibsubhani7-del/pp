3. Device Identifiers & App Set ID Compliance

In strict compliance with Google Play Store User Data rules:

  • No Persistent Identifier Linking: Our App does not access or link persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI, IMSI, MAC address, or SIM Serial Number) to personal user data or resettable identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID).
  • App Set ID Policy: Any App Set ID collected is used exclusively for internal diagnostics, aggregated analytics, and fraud mitigation. It is never linked to advertising identifiers or used for personalized ads.

7. Data Security & Encryption Practices

We enforce robust technical safeguards to ensure that personal and sensitive data is handled securely:

  • Modern Cryptography: All data transmitted between the App and our backend infrastructure is encrypted using modern protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Data at Rest: Sensitive databases are encrypted using AES-256 with restricted, audited access credentials.

8. User Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, users have full autonomy to delete their accounts and permanently erase all associated personal data at any time. We permanently delete your data rather than simply deactivating or freezing your account.

Option A: In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open [Your App Name] on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Account.
  3. Select Delete Account & Data and confirm.

Option B: External Web Deletion Request (Play Console Web Link)

If you uninstalled the application or wish to delete your records online, submit a request directly to our privacy desk:

Request Permanent Account Deletion

*All associated personal records will be completely expunged within 30 days of verification, in accordance with applicable legal retention requirements.
